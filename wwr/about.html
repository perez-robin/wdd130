Our mission is to deliver safe, high-quality, and memorable outdoor white water adventures while fostering river conservation and environmental awareness for all guests.

Our History

Founded in 2010, Aura River Expeditions began with a dedication to safety and exceptional river experiences. Over the past decade, our certified guides have safely led over 50,000 guests down premier river routes with an unblemished safety record.
